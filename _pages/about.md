I am Lezhi Chen (陈乐知), currently an undergraduate student at Sichuan University. 

My research interests lie primarily at the intersection between **living systems** and **machine learning**, with a recent focus on *single-cell spatiotemporal dynamics*. I am committed to developing scalable, actionable, and reliable computational methods to accelerate scientific discovery, while also drawing on insights from *statistical physics* and *applied mathematics* (e.g. optimal transport, dynamical modeling) to gain a deeper understanding of living systems.
